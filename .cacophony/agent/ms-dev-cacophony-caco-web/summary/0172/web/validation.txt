WHY:
- 300ms double-tap-zoom delay is the most common
  mobile-tap perceived-latency cost.
- iOS Safari and older Android reserve 300ms after first
  tap to wait for possible second tap (double-tap zoom).
- touch-action: manipulation tells browser the element
  does not need double-tap-zoom, only pan + pinch.
- Browser dispatches click immediately on touchend.
- Backward-compatible: panning still works.

OUT OF SCOPE:
- input[type=text], textarea -- need text-selection drag
  behavior on touch (default touch-action: auto).
- Workspace splitter -- has touch-action: none for
  drag-only semantics.
