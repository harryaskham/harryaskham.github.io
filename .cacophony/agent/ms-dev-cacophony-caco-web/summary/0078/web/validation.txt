  any without aria-live= in the tag body.

Why role="status" alone is insufficient:
- role="status" is an aria-live=polite implicit container
  in the WAI-ARIA spec, BUT many assistive-tech setups
  treat it as a hint, not as guaranteed announcement
  on DOM mutation. Explicit aria-live="polite" works
  across more AT configurations.
